# Mango's Mealtime — Right4Paws Ultimate Diet

Style: Crayon Picture Book · 1080×1920 (9:16) · 24 fps · ~40 s · English VO (playful male dog, mild British)

## Brief (summed up)

- Product: Right4Paws **Ultimate Diet – Medium & Maxi Adult**. Tagline "Raw Made Right".
- Claims used on screen/VO (all from the product page): fresh chicken (67.5%), fresh eggs (16%), seasonal veggies & fruits (11.58%), whole sardines (2.2%); "Irresistible freshness … wins over even the pickiest eaters"; gently dehydrated flakes.
- POV: the dog. Before = fussy, bored of the same brown pellets. After = loves eating, waits for every meal.
- Brand look (from the site): signal red ≈ `#E0262B`, warm cream ≈ `#FBEFE4`, near-black text, white. Headings a heavy rounded sans (→ Fredoka Bold); the "Raw Made Right" line is a brush-script hand (→ Kalam Bold). Red swoosh corners on every graphic.
- Music: classical, public domain. Sound: full sound design, VO, score.

## Three candidate structures

1. **Colouring-book before/after.** The page starts as outlines only (the dull "before" life is uncoloured). The red box arrives and colour floods in, object by object, until Mango's whole world is coloured. *Chosen*: colour = appetite, so the product literally brings the page to life, and "colouring is an action" is the style's own grammar.
2. **A week of page turns.** Monday to Sunday, one page per day, the bowl getting emptier faster. Clear, but page turns every 5 s get choppy and fight the "merge into one another" brief.
3. **Two crayons argue.** A brown crayon (kibble) and a red crayon (Right4Paws) fight over the page. Fun, but the dog becomes a bystander and the POV gets lost.

Why 1: one continuous tall page means the camera can glide from beat to beat with no cuts, which is exactly the "animations merge into one another" request. Only one page turn, for the time jump to "every morning".

## Logline and arc

A fussy dog named Mango, bored of the same brown pellets, finds his world colouring itself in when a big red box arrives, and now he counts the minutes to every meal.

- **Setup** (0–10 s): grey, outline-only page; Mango chin-on-paws, turning his nose up at pellets.
- **Turn** (10–20 s): the red box drops in, scribbled red; chicken, eggs, veggies, sardines march out, each one coloured in on a beat, and Mango colours in with them (ginger fur arriving = appetite arriving).
- **Ending** (20–40 s): page turn to "every morning": Mango waits nicely by his red bowl, the bowl fills, he dives in. A brand-red wash sweeps the page and reveals the white-wax "Raw Made Right" hidden there since frame one; pull out to the page taped to a fridge beside the pack.

## The dog

**Mango**, a ginger **Indian Pariah ("Indie")** dog, male, ~3 years old, lanky and springy. The breed is on the product's own breed chart, and an Indie fits an Indian brand better than another golden retriever. Huge ears, one up, one flopped. A curly question-mark tail he can't control. White blaze on chest, red collar with a little paw tag. Voice: cheeky, bouncy, mildly British, a bit theatrical about food.

## Benchmark

- **Pixar/Hallmark-style dog-POV shorts and the John Lewis Christmas ads (grammar only)**: one animal, one want, the joke told through the eyes; the emotion carried by the music, not by the words.
- **Learn**: big readable poses; the cut on the music; holding a beat of silence before the payoff; the product shown as the turn, not a lecture.
- **Don't take**: characters, music, specific shots, any brand marks other than Right4Paws.

## Camera plan: one tall page

The whole film is drawn on **one tall picture-book page** (about 1080 × 5400 page units) plus one page turn. The camera never cuts; it pushes, pulls, trucks and tilts down the page, and the next scene is always already being drawn just outside the frame, so each beat flows into the next.

## Score and cue map

- **Piece**: J. S. Bach-era **Minuet in G major (BWV Anh. 114, Petzold)**, public domain, re-arranged in-house (no recording used).
- **Instruments** (the style's palette, kept classical as a chamber arrangement): clarinet (melody), nylon guitar (bass line), celesta/glockenspiel (sparkles on each coloured ingredient), pizzicato-style plucked bass, light shaker. No grand piano, no string pad.
- **Tempo**: ♩ = 120, 3/4 → 1 bar = 1.5 s.
- 0–10 s (bars 1–6): **"before"**: melody alone on bassoon-ish clarinet low, slightly lazy, dry.
- 10 s: box thump on beat 1 of bar 7; full minuet A section enters, clarinet up an octave, guitar bass.
- 14–20 s: one glockenspiel note per ingredient, on beats.
- 20 s: page-turn whoosh; B section (D major colour) begins, lighter.
- 26.5–28 s: **music stops**, one beat of silence + a clock tick.
- 28 s: pour sound, then music returns full on the downbeat.
- 33–40 s: final A phrase, broad ritardando, music-box ping on the logo; ends on a G-major chord at 39.5.
