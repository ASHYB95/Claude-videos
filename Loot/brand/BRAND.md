# LOOT — Brand Guidelines

Source: `Branding_A.pdf` (12 slides). This file is the text version, so builds can use it without opening the PDF.

## The brand in one line
**LOOT — Secure the bag.** *The Anti-Agency Marketplace.*

A marketplace where brands post paid video "missions" and everyday creators ("hunters") pick them up, film them and get paid.

## Story
- **The old guard is broken.** Agencies sell "perfect". Gen Z doesn't trust perfect. They trust raw, messy and real.
- **The disconnect:** brands need volume; creators have the talent but no access.
- **We are the glitch in the system.** "A student in a dorm room with a smartphone is more powerful than a Madison Avenue boardroom."
- **Stance:** not one influencer with a million followers — a million creators with one mission.
- **Vision:** to democratize influence.
- **We don't hire employees. We unleash hunters.**
  - *For creators:* Monetize your existence. No boss. No schedule. No selling your soul. Just the mission.
  - *For brands:* Raw, authentic firepower for challenger brands to out-noise the giants.

## Personality: The Digital Outlaw
- **Vibe:** "Think Grand Theft Auto meets Fintech."
- **Our world:** not a tech company. A gaming lobby. A streetwear drop.
- **Language:** hustle-efficient, high-energy, slightly dangerous.
- **We are:** UNAPOLOGETIC · DIRECT · ELECTRIC
- **We are NOT:** Corporate, Polite, Safe.

## Voice: speak the language
| ❌ Corporate way | ✅ LOOT way |
| --- | --- |
| Payment processed successfully. | **Bag Secured.** |
| Please submit your video for review. | **Drop the Loot.** |
| New task available. | **New Mission: Unlocked.** |
| Our platform connects brands with users. | **We arm the rebels.** |
| Terms and conditions apply. | **Read the Rules. Don't get banned.** |

App vocabulary: *missions, contracts, hunters, codename, passkey, bag, stash, loot, drop, transmit, console, protocol, mainframe*.

## Colour: Mint & Slate — "The Modern Heist"
Cool pastels to signal wealth, modernity and "Gen Z luxury". No aggressive reds/yellows.

| Name | Hex | Meaning | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Digital Mint** — "The Win" | `#98FF98` | Money, go, freshness | CTAs, wins, payouts, highlights, the money-bag icon |
| **Slate** — "The System" | `#7B7D9E` | Technology, structure | Secondary type, rules, diagrams, chart bars |
| **Off-white** — "The Space" | `#F5F5F5` | Clean, expensive, open | Backgrounds |
| Ink (from slides/app) | `#111111` | — | Headlines, black buttons |

In the app, error/rejected states use a soft red; keep it for those states only.

## Type: "Our mission shouts. Our code is precise."
| Role | Font | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The Shout** | Druk Wide (commercial) | ALL CAPS. Takes up space. Shouts the mission. |
| **The Code** | Space Mono (free, Google Fonts) | Looks like code. Technical. Precise. e.g. `Mission_ID: 7B7D9E` / `Objective: Authenticity` / `Status: Pending_Drop` |

- The slides mix a condensed heavy cut (SECURE THE BAG, THE OLD GUARD IS BROKEN) with an extended heavy cut (THE DIGITAL OUTLAW, MISSION PROTOCOL).
- The **app** uses a heavy grotesk (Inter Black-like, tight tracking) for headings and a monospace for labels.
- Free look-alikes for video work: condensed shout → **Anton**; extended shout → **Archivo Expanded Black** / **Unbounded**; code → **Space Mono**; app headings → **Inter Black**.

## The mark
1. **Symbol** — an open box / corner bracket `L`: the framing of a shot, or the capture of loot.
2. **Wordmark** — `LOOT`: heavy, thick, blocky letters. Uncompromising and confident.
3. **Icon** — a flat money-bag glyph with `$` in Digital Mint: the target and the prize.
- Hero lockup: the bracket `L` sits behind/below-left of `LOOT`.
- Signature graphic: a **mint torn-paper slash** ripping diagonally through `LOOT` (cover slide); jagged mint tear as a divider (slide 2).

## Visual devices
- Corner brackets / HUD frames (`OBJECTIVE: SECURE THE BAG`), mission IDs, status lines in mono.
- Isometric "arsenal" diagrams in slate line-work.
- Line icons in mint (phone, rocket).
- Lots of off-white space; black type; mint used sparingly as the "win".

## Sign-off
**THE BRIEF IS SIMPLE. SECURE THE BAG.**
