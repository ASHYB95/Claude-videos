## Where every claim comes from (all from the app screens or the brand guide)
- Paid video missions, guaranteed reward, spots left: *Loot* tab and *Mission detail*.
- Two tiers, Beginner ₹3,000 / Advanced ₹5,000; beginner reshoots only for technical glitches, verified by the LOOT team; "No creative changes allowed!": onboarding tutorial 4.
- Money pre-funded by the brand and held ("Escrow guarantee", "₹5,000 securely held"): onboarding tutorial 2.
- Hooks, body angles, CTAs, scene-by-scene, teleprompter, do's and don'ts: *Mission brief* screens.
- Admin approval unlocks the app: *Home, pending approval*.
- Studio console, Transmit to brand, revision required, approved & paid: *Studio* screens.
- Stash balance, Withdraw funds, "Withdrawal to UPI": *Profile & Stash*.
- "No boss. No schedule. … Just the mission.", "Read the rules. Don't get banned.", "Bag secured", "Drop the loot", "Secure the bag": brand guide.
- Amounts on screen (₹4,500, ₹48,500 etc.) are the app's demo data; the VO never states a number.
