## Build notes
- Phone screens are the app's real screenshots (`reference/screens/`, copied from `Loot/assets/creator-app`);
  pop-outs are crops of the same screens (`assets/pops/`). The escrow, pending banner, answer cards and
  rule card are rebuilt in the app's style (escrow shows ₹4,500 to match the mission; the tutorial screen says ₹5,000).
- Cleaned for publishing: `home-clean.png` (green feed area → slate placeholder with a play button),
  `console-ready.png` (real company filename → `take_01.mp4`). Real-person/real-brand screens not used.
- Brand graphics are generated by `tools/gen_brand.py` (torn slash, money bag, torn wipe panels).
- Tap points are in screenshot pixels (1179×2556) × 568/1179 into the phone screen.
- GSAP gotcha hit here: a `fromTo` that sets `autoAlpha` only in its *from* vars does not keep it when
  the timeline plays forward from before it; put `autoAlpha` in both (fixed in `showV`, `slashGrow`, `wipe`).
- Storyboard frames: `storyboard/frames.html` → `node tools/shoot_frames.cjs` → `node tools/shoot_sheet.cjs`.
