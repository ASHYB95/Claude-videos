# Delulu Check — "Your chat already knows"

Style: Living Screencast · 1080×1920 (9:16) · 30 fps · 30 s · English VO (offline, warm young female) · upbeat R&B
Goal: brand awareness for a cold audience, by showing (not telling) what the app does in one relatable situation.

## Brief (summed up)

- Product: **Delulu Check** (delulucheck.com). Upload a WhatsApp/Instagram chat → it reads every message → a report on where you stand (Vibe Check, Interest Level, Timeline of Doom, Behavioral Profiles) → **Doctor Delulu** to ask what to do → the **Delulu Keyboard** (God Mode, Reply, Psychic, 12 tones) to write the reply in any app.
- Claims used (all from the site): "Upload your WhatsApp or Instagram chat. We read every message and tell you where you stand." · "412 messages analyzed" (their demo) · Interest Level 62, "Lukewarm affection" · "They want the feeling, not the responsibility." · Doctor Delulu replies (their demo text) · keyboard modes GOD MODE / COMPOSE / REPLY / PSYCHIC / RIZZ, tones FUNNY / FLIRTY / SAVAGE / SWEET / CHILL · "Your friends are too nice to tell you. Your chat already did." · "50,000+ people" · "Free preview on your first chat".
- Brand: near-black `#0b0a10` with a deep plum glow `#2a0f2e`; signature gradient magenta `#c02ee0` → violet `#7a4cf0` → blue `#3b63f0` (buttons, user bubbles); hot pink `#ff2e88` and neon yellow `#ffc928` accents in the report UI. Type: tight heavy grotesk for headlines (→ Inter Tight/Inter Bold, tracked −4%), condensed caps for buttons and labels (→ Bebas Neue / Oswald look-alike), mono letter-spaced eyebrows (→ JetBrains Mono), condensed heavy italic for report titles (→ Anton italic look-alike).

## Three candidate structures

1. **One situation, start to finish.** A single relatable moment ("he went quiet for 3 days, then sent a meme") is carried through the whole product: upload → report → Doctor Delulu → keyboard reply. *Chosen*: a cold viewer learns the whole loop through one story they already recognise; each feature answers the question the previous beat raised.
2. **Feature list with chapter pills (01–04).** Clear, but it reads like an ad deck; a cold audience scrolls past lists.
3. **The race.** Split screen: "asking the group chat" vs "asking Delulu Check". Funny, but halves the screen on a phone-sized vertical frame and pushes the product UI too small to read.

## Logline and arc

She's spiralling over a three-day silence and a meme; Delulu Check reads the whole chat, tells her the truth, and hands her the perfect reply.

- **Hook (0–4 s)**: the chat — 3 days of silence, then a meme. The viewer has been here.
- **Turn (4–19 s)**: upload → the mascot reads every message → Interest Level counts up to 62, "lukewarm affection" → Doctor Delulu: "let him make the plan".
- **Ending (19–30 s)**: back in the chat, the Delulu Keyboard writes a calm reply; send. End on the brand line, logo, CTA.

## The mascot

**Delu**: the Delulu Check "DC + heart" mark redrawn as a 14×12 pixel sprite (magenta → violet heart body, two pixel eyes, stubby legs). Lives on top edges of real UI rows. It is the app's agency: it runs along message rows while reading, hoists the "62" meter, sits on Doctor Delulu's bubble, and rides the sent message out.

## Benchmark

- **Screen Studio-style app recordings + Apple Guided Tours (grammar)**: cursor-led push-ins, one feature per sentence, a calm voice over a lively UI.
- **TikTok "storytime" app ads (grammar)**: problem in the first second, captions always on, payoff before 20 s.
- Learn: the zoom always lands where the next sentence points; the UI really changes on every action.
- Don't take: any other app's UI, characters, music.

## Score and cue map

- Original upbeat R&B, **92 BPM**, 4/4 (bar ≈ 2.61 s), key of F minor (Fm9 – B♭m9 – E♭maj9 – A♭maj7).
- Instruments: electric-piano chords (synth Rhodes), sub/fingered bass, tight kit (kick, snare with clap, swung 16th hats), finger snaps; **chiptune pulse voice = Delu** (hops on the words it lands on), as the style asks (hi-fi band = the world, chiptune = the mascot).
- 0–4: keys + snaps only (filtered), the hook.
- 4.0: drop — full groove on the whip.
- 11.0–11.5: drums drop out, one held pad + riser, **silence beat** before "62".
- 11.5: groove back, chiptune arpeggio as the meter counts.
- 19.5: tom fill + crash at the chapter seam into the keyboard.
- 25.0: breakdown to keys + bass under the hero line; final hit on the logo at 28.0; ring out.
