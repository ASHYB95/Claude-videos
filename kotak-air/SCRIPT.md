# Kotak Air Credit Card — "Cleared for Take-off" (concept + script v1)

## Concept

**What it is:** a pre-flight briefing for the Kotak credit card sales team. The video is styled like an airport: each feature is a "gate" the viewer passes through, numbers flip in on a split-flap departures board, and the fees, rules and cheat sheet sit on boarding passes.

**Why this works for sales training:** think of a pilot's pre-flight checklist. It's short, it's in a fixed order and nothing gets skipped. Reps should leave with (1) a one-line pitch, (2) every number they'll be asked about, (3) the conditions they must disclose so they don't mis-sell, and (4) one value story that closes the sale.

## Open points

1. **"No Joining Fee!" vs ₹999.** The page banner says "Kotak Air Credit Card – No Joining Fee!", but the fees table, FAQ and T&C PDF all say joining fee ₹999 + GST with no waiver, and the welcome miles are "upon payment of joining fees". The script uses ₹999 + GST. If a no-joining-fee offer is running, Kotak should confirm its terms and how it affects the welcome miles.
2. **"Who to pitch" is our inference.** It comes from the product's mechanics (₹60,000 a quarter ≈ ₹20,000 a month to unlock lounges). It is not Kotak's stated target segment, so Kotak should confirm or swap in their own segment.
3. The ₹16,500 example assumes Kotak's own ₹2,000 per lounge visit. It is shown with its source note.
