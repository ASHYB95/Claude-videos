## Open points
1. **"No Joining Fee!" vs ₹999.** The page banner says "No Joining Fee!", but the fees table, FAQ and T&C say ₹999 + GST with no waiver. The script uses ₹999 + GST until Kotak confirms.
2. **Section 10 is our inference.** It comes from the ₹60,000-per-quarter lounge rule and is not Kotak's stated segment. Kotak should confirm it or replace it.
