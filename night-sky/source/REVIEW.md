# Review — The Night Sky Is a Museum (28.8 s, 1080×1920)

## Creative calls
- Storyline built: talking head (hook + payoff on camera) with the middle carried by Vox-style paper-collage plates and two Higgs B-roll shots.
  Not built: a pure-collage explainer with no presenter; a single continuous "camera rises from rooftop to galaxy" shot.
- Presenter, voice (ElevenLabs "Jessica") and look were chosen without a brief; all swappable.
- A-roll only lip-syncs the on-camera lines (≈12 s of voice) to halve the Higgs Intone cost (240 vs 464 credits).
  Intone stretched speech ~9–19 % per sentence; each on-camera shot plays the A-roll at the fitted rate
  (1.09 / 1.145 / 1.14×) from an offset found by envelope correlation (r = 0.95–0.99), so lips follow the tighter VO.
- Era years on the stars are illustrative (unnamed stars), stated in CREDITS.

## Measured
- `hyperframes check`: ok. Kept warnings: captions track has 23 groups (one host by design); WebGL ReadPixels console notes.
- Audio (rendered file): −14.7 LUFS integrated, −1.1 dBTP, no clipping. Measured, not listened to.
- Lip-sync offsets, star drift (phase correlation, ~48 px/s →, 92 px/s ↓) and SFX peak times were measured; caption timing comes from ElevenLabs word alignment.

## Weakest shot / with more time
- The ladder plate (10.6–16 s) has empty upper-left paper in the Earth/Moon framing; a "NOT TO SCALE" note or a tighter layout would fill it.
- Museum end card leaves the bottom 40 % as bare paper (kept clear for Reels UI).
- The A-roll at 1.14× makes head motion slightly brisk; not visible at phone size in the frames checked.
