## Look

Vox-style collage: navy construction paper, cream sticker-bordered photo cut-outs with drop shadows, Courier typewriter tags,
highlighter yellow (#FFD43B) only on the number that matters and on moving light, annotation red (#D7372B) for circles, stamps and strikes,
film grain, 2.5D parallax on printed star specks. Captions in Barlow 800 with the keyword on a yellow highlighter band.
Full numbers in `source/SPEC.md`.

## Facts used

Speed of light ≈ 299,792 km/s (≈ 7.48 × Earth's 40,075 km circumference per second). Moon ≈ 1.3 light-seconds, Sun ≈ 8 min 20 s.
Naked-eye stars range from a few to a few thousand light-years.

## Rebuild

`cd source && npx hyperframes@0.9.20 render -o ../night-sky.mp4 --quality delivery` (Chrome + FFmpeg). Captions are generated by
`python3 work/gen_captions.py` from `work/words.json`; the VO pause-tightening is `work/tighten.py`.
