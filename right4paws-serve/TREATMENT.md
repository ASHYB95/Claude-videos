# Mango's Dinner Drill — How to Serve Right4Paws Ultimate Diet

Style: Mid-century Cartoon (`midcentury-toon`) **in Right4Paws brand colours** · 1080×1920 (9:16) · 24 fps · 46 s · English VO (ElevenLabs "Bill", a mature American announcer) · burned-in captions + `.srt`

## Brief (summed up)

- **Job**: a "how to serve" film for **Right4Paws Ultimate Diet – Medium & Maxi Adult**. The viewer should come away able to portion and serve it.
- **Facts**: only from the brand's own product page and Feeding Guide (see `FACTS.md`): weigh your dog → a 30 kg dog may need ~350 g a day; one 250 ml cup ≈ 100 g of flakes; serve dry, sprinkle warm water for extra flavour and aroma, or mash; more nutrient-dense than kibble, so you feed less (25–30 % per bowl).
- **Brand**: the client asked for brand colours. Palette taken from the pack, the logo tab and the site; the red/white checker floor and halftone dot rings come from the Feeding Guide cover; the real logo tab (rendered from the Feeding Guide PDF) hangs from the top of the end card exactly as on the guide's cover.
- **Hero dog**: **Mango**, the ginger Indie from the earlier Right4Paws film, redrawn as a 1950s geometric cartoon. Natural colours stay natural (his fur, the food, the steel bowl); his collar is brand red.
- Workflow: script → storyboard (approved) → film.

## Three candidate structures

1. **The set-up card (chosen).** Mango drops his empty bowl; a numbered list stamps on; three steps (*Weigh · Measure · Serve*), each one hand action on a beat; a silence, the bowl lands, a pro tip, then the brand's own cover as the end card. It is the style's own use case (a set-up guide) and it teaches the job in order.
2. **Result first.** Open on three finished bowls, then rewind "here's how". Pretty, but gives the payoff away and loses the portion maths.
3. **Do / don't split.** Every step shown right and wrong. Clear, but doubles the length and turns a friendly product into a list of mistakes.

## Logline and arc

Mango already knows the drill: a calm 1950s narrator walks his human through serving Ultimate Diet in three steps, while Mango supervises.

- **Setup** (0–8 s): Mango drops his empty bowl (clang). "This is Mango. And this… is dinner." The pack thumps down on "dinner". Red title card, ① ② ③ stamp on.
- **Turn** (8–32 s): Step 1 weigh (Mango hops on a scale, the needle swings, the music drops out, 30 kg *ding*). Step 2 measure (one cup ≈ 100 g, a finger flicks it level). Step 3 serve it your way: three colour-block panels — dry / warm water / mashed.
- **Ending** (32–46 s): total silence while Mango waits dead still; the bowl touches the floor (*clank*); the band comes back a whole step up, Mango dives in (CRUNCH!). Pro tip card: feed less. End card: the brand's own cover, held still.

## Benchmark

- **1950s classroom how-to films** and **mid-century recipe cards / appliance manuals** (grammar only): direct address, one sentence per picture, numbered steps, a held summary card. **The Right4Paws Feeding Guide** for the brand's visual language (logo tab, checker floor, dot rings).
- **Learn**: one product colour all the way through; the hand *is* the step; a numbered list that opens item by item; holding the final card still long enough to screenshot.
- **Don't take**: characters, drawings, lettering, jingles, any marks except Right4Paws'.

## Score and cue map

- **Original West-Coast cool-jazz combo** (`source/music/score.py`): vibraphone, flute (an original two-bar theme), walking jazz bass, brushes, ride (MuldjordKit), celesta, finger snaps. ♩ = 120, 4/4, bar = 2 s, 23 bars.
- 0–4: snaps + walking bass; vib stab + kick on the pack (2.5). 4–8: band in, theme under the narration, a vibraphone note per medallion, a glissando into the iris (7.5).
- **Each step has its own vibraphone chord**: F6/9 (8.0), B♭maj7 (16.0), Gm9 (22.0). Music cut 11.5–12.0, celesta *ding* on 30 kg (12.0). Celesta + vib run as each serve panel lights (24.5 / 26.5 / 30.0). Bar 16 hangs on C7sus and stops dead at 32.0.
- 32.0–33.6 **total silence** (room tone cut too); the clank is the first sound after it.
- 34.0: **up a whole step to G**, crash, sticks on the ride, theme in G. A flute slide down on the arrow (37.2). End: theme reprise, then the three step chords return one per beat a step up (G6/9, Cmaj7, Am9 at 42.0 / 42.5 / 43.0), final G6/9 rings out.

## Not the demo again

Compared with the style's demo (*Meet Pip*): structure (a dog-led serving guide with a silence payoff vs a presenter-led set-up guide with a route-pattern payoff) — different; opening (a dog dropping his bowl vs a box opening) — different; signature shot (three-panel split frame vs a pull-back over a floor plan) — different; camera path (colour-block wipes + one iris vs an iris at every step) — different; score shape (snaps-only hook, a half-bar cut, a total silence before the clank, summary chords) — partly shared (key change up a step at the payoff); ending (the brand's own cover, held still vs an iris-out) — different. Five of six differ.
