# Mango's Dinner Drill — How to Serve Right4Paws Ultimate Diet

Style: Mid-century Cartoon (`midcentury-toon`) · 1080×1920 (9:16) · 24 fps · ~42 s · English VO (calm 1950s classroom narrator, male, ElevenLabs) · burned-in captions + `.srt`

## Brief (summed up)

- **Job**: a "how to serve" film for **Right4Paws Ultimate Diet – Medium & Maxi Adult**. The viewer should come away able to portion and serve it.
- **Facts**: only from the brand's own pages and Feeding Guide (see `FACTS.md`): weigh your dog → 30 kg ≈ 350 g/day; one 250 ml cup ≈ 100 g of flakes; serve dry, sprinkle warm water for extra flavour and aroma, or mash; more nutrient-dense than kibble, so you feed less (25–30 %).
- **Brand**: logo from right4paws.com on the end card; the pack (red + cream, "ULTIMATE DIET") redrawn in the cartoon style; tagline "Raw Made Right"; URL right4paws.com.
- **Hero dog**: **Mango**, the ginger Indie from the earlier Right4Paws film, redrawn as a 1950s geometric cartoon (brand continuity).
- Storyboard review: **yes** (script → storyboard → final).

## Three candidate structures

1. **The set-up card (chosen).** A dog pushes an empty bowl into frame; a numbered list stamps on; then three steps (*Weigh · Measure · Serve*), each one hand action on a beat, ending in one held summary. It is the style's own use case (a set-up guide), and it teaches the job in order.
2. **Result first.** Open on three finished bowls (dry, soft, mashed), then rewind "here's how". Pretty, but it gives the payoff away in second one and the portion maths gets lost.
3. **Do / don't split.** Every step shown twice, right and wrong (heaped cup vs. level cup). Clear, but it doubles the screen time and turns a friendly product into a list of mistakes.

Why 1: one dog, one want (dinner), one turn (the bowl goes down), and every step lands on a beat. It also gives the film a gag readable at thumbnail size: a dog who already knows the drill.

## Logline and arc

Mango already knows the drill: a calm 1950s narrator walks his human through serving Ultimate Diet in three steps, while Mango supervises.

- **Setup** (0–8 s): Mango shoves his empty bowl into frame. "This is Mango. And this… is dinner." The pack thumps down. Three numbered medallions stamp on.
- **Turn** (8–30 s): Step 1 weigh (Mango hops on a scale, dial swings to 30 kg). Step 2 measure (one cup ≈ 100 g). Step 3 serve it your way: the frame splits into three colour blocks: dry / warm-water / mashed.
- **Ending** (30–42 s): total silence; Mango sits perfectly still; the bowl touches the floor (*clink*): music back up a step, Mango dives in. Pro tip, then a dead-still end card: pack, logo, Mango with a full belly.

## Benchmark

## Score and cue map

- **Original West-Coast cool-jazz combo**: vibraphone (chords, glissandi), flute (melody), pizzicato walking bass, brushes, finger snaps, celesta sparkles. No piano, no strings.
- **Tempo** ♩ = 120, 4/4 → 1 beat = 0.5 s, 1 bar = 2 s. Every cut, stamp and hand action lands on a beat.
- 0–4 s (bars 1–2): snaps + walking bass only, a sly vamp; the pack thump on bar 2 beat 1 (2.0 s).
- 4–8 s (bars 3–4): flute melody enters; three medallion stamps on beats (6.0, 6.5, 7.0); vibraphone gliss into the iris at 8.0.
- 8–16 s (bars 5–8): **Step 1 chord** (vibes, F maj7). Scale jump on 10.0; needle swing 10.5–12.0; **half-bar of silence** 12.0–13.0; *ding* + "30 kg" on 13.0.
- 16–22 s (bars 9–11): **Step 2 chord** (G min7); scoop on 18.0, level flick on 19.0.
- 22–30 s (bars 12–15): **Step 3 chord** (A min7 → D7); each band lights on a downbeat (24.5, 26.5, 28.5); celesta sparkle on each.
- 30.0–31.6: **total silence** (room tone cut too). 31.6: *clink* (bowl down).
- 32–36 s (bars 17–18): music back **up a whole step**, brushes switch to sticks on the ride; crunch-munch on the beat.
- 36–42 s (bars 19–21): flute reprise; on 39.0 the three step chords return one per beat (summary), last chord rings to 42.0; mantel-clock tick alone under the final hold.
